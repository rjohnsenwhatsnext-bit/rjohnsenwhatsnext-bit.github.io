How to play

Guess today's word. Each guess must be a real word of the right length. After each guess the tiles show how close you were:

G Green ◆ · right letter, right spot

O Gold • · right letter, another spot

X Slate – · not in the word

Three puzzles a day: Easy (5 letters, 6 tries), Medium (6 letters, 5 tries) and Hard (7 letters, 5 tries). New words at midnight.
