MAKE YOURSELF COMFORTABLE

Settings.

Tap anywhere on a line. The arrow shows its exit direction. If it cannot move, the coral highlight shows what is in the way. Rocks stay put. Frozen lines need a clear path before the first tap thaws them. The next tap slides them out. Number circles clear in ascending order. A lock opens when the key line with its matching letter slides out. Undoing a key closes its locks again. Twin lines carry matching letter tags. Tap either twin to slide both out when both paths are clear. Twins can pass through each other. Undo restores both together. Fuse badges mark bombs: when one slides out, it pops one touching line, even if that line is blocked, frozen or locked. If several touch, the lowest line ID is chosen, not the number-circle order. A popped bomb does not trigger another pop. Focus a bomb to outline its current target. With no touching neighbour, it simply slides out. Undo restores both the bomb and its popped neighbour.
