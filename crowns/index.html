How to play

Put one 👑 in every row, every column and every colour.

Crowns can never touch, not even corner to corner.

Tap a square once to mark it ✕ (no crown here), twice for a 👑, three times to clear it. Auto X marks the squares a crown rules out for you.

A new puzzle every day at midnight, the same for everyone. Every puzzle has one answer and can be solved without guessing.
