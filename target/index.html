    How to play

    Make the target from the six numbers.

    Tap a number, tap + − × or ÷, then tap another number. The answer takes their place. Keep going until you hit the target exactly.

    Whole numbers only, nothing below one, and you do not have to use them all. A new puzzle every day at midnight, the same for everyone.
