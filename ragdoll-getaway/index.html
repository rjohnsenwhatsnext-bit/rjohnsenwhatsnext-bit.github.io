THE PLAN IS SIMPLE

Get to the yellow van.

  1. MOVEDrag the left pad, or hold the arrow buttons.
  2. BLASTYour blaster automatically aims at nearby robots and breakable scenery.
  3. JUMP / DIVEJump the gaps. Dive to dodge shots and tumble through the chaos. Jump releases a rope.
  4. THREE STARSEscape. Grab all three loot bags. Keep at least two shields.

Keyboard: A/D or arrows to move. W/Space to jump. S to dive. Escape to pause. Landscape gives you a wider view.
